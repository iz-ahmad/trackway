  • A masthead that orients instead of a thin line of type

  • Filtered-out records are removed, never faded
  • Search says what it searches

Masthead & Contents

index first

The page opens as a real index. A proper masthead carries the wordmark, the search field and four figures on a rule. Below it, Contents: every topic as a table row with its own spread of kinds, so you pick a topic before you read anything. Filters are outlined buttons that fill with their own colour when on, and turning one off removes those records.

Working Rail

controls always visible

The header shrinks to a bar. Every control moves into a permanent left rail: four real checkboxes with counts, then the topic list.
